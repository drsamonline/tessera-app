# Commercial licensing

The TESSERA console is **dual-licensed**, the same as the engine.

| Use | Licence | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Personal projects, evaluation, research, teaching, internal trials that do not generate revenue | [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE) | Free |
| Anything commercial: a revenue-generating deployment, hosting it for customers, bundling it into something you sell | **This document** | By agreement |

PolyForm Noncommercial restricts *users*, not the licensor, so the copyright holder may offer the same code on different
terms. Nothing about the free licence changes.

## What a commercial licence gives you

- Use, modify and deploy this in a commercial setting, including inside a product you sell.
- The right to keep your modifications private.
- Written terms you can show your own procurement team.

## What it does not give you

- **No exclusivity.** The same terms are available to anyone.
- **No trademark rights.** "TESSERA" as a mark is not licensed.
- **No warranty.** As is, as the free licence says.
- **No support commitment** unless a separate agreement says so.

## The one thing to understand about this app

It stores whatever you put in it, in a browser. If you deploy it for a client, **you** are the Data Fiduciary for that
data — see [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). A licence does not transfer that obligation, and no licence can.

## What a lawyer must confirm

- Enforceability of the dual structure in your jurisdiction.
- Whether your intended free tier really sits inside the noncommercial grant.
- The trademark position for the name.
