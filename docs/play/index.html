      About

      TESSERA console is a working demonstration of the reference core at drsamonline/tessera: field-level merge, conflict resolution, a hash-chained ledger, seat-based routing and the proposal gate - all reimplemented in the browser, with no server and no network.

      Built by Dr Sohil Momin. Licensed under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. If it saved you an afternoon, buy the author a coffee.

      Deal
      Tombstone this record

      The record stops being served and the reason is kept next to the tombstone, forever. Nothing is destroyed — the ledger keeps what was written, because an append-only chain cannot un-write a hash.

      Log an email

      Logged as a ledger event, not sent. TESSERA keeps the record of the conversation; your mail client still sends the mail.

      Edit field

      New customer

      Consent, lawful basis and purpose are required, and they are not defaulted — a record created with an invented lawful basis is a compliance claim nobody made.

      Import customers

      Paste a CSV with a header row, or choose a file. Nothing is written until you press Import, and an import can be undone in one press.
